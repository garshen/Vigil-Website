Frequently Asked Questions

How does VIGIL work offline? +
VIGIL uses a local-first architecture where all data is stored locally on the officer's device. When internet is available, VIGIL automatically syncs all changes to the cloud. This means officers can continue reporting incidents, patrols, and apprehensions even in areas with poor connectivity.
What platforms does VIGIL support? +
VIGIL is available on iOS, Android, and Web. Officers can use their existing smartphones, and managers can access the command dashboard from any device with internet access.
Is VIGIL PSIRA compliant? +
Yes, VIGIL is fully designed to align with PSIRA requirements for South African private security companies. We include support for all security grades, ranks, and divisions recognized by PSIRA.
How secure is the photo evidence storage? +
Photos are stored in AWS S3 with end-to-end encryption. Access is controlled through role-based permissions, and there's a complete audit trail of who accessed what photos and when.
Can VIGIL integrate with SAPS systems? +
VIGIL captures and manages SAPS case numbers and officer details. We're working on deeper SAPS API integrations. Contact our sales team for current integration capabilities.
What kind of support do you provide? +
Support varies by plan. Starter plans get email support, Professional gets priority email/phone support, and Enterprise gets dedicated account managers and custom training.
